OUR STORY

Why we created this page

Our child is a wonderful and unique person with their own strengths, interests, challenges, and dreams. Like many families, we are learning every day how to provide the right support.

Support can take many forms — therapies, educational resources, communication tools, sensory supports, community activities, and caring people who understand our journey.

We hope this website helps us connect with people who would like to support our family in meaningful ways.
